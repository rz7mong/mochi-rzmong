# Mochi rzmong: LBWK-style sticker sheet

Original fan-made livery (white / black / red) for the Mochi case. **Inspired by LBWK (Liberty Walk) / F40 style. Not official.** It contains no official Liberty Walk, Ferrari or Dasai logos, and all sponsor names (KUMO FUEL, PIXEL GRIP, RZMONG WORKS, DESK GT) are made up.

Sizes were measured on the v3 smoothed case (63.9 × 93.5 × 53.4 mm).

## Printing

- Print at **100 % / "Actual size"**, not "Fit to page". Check that the 50 mm bar measures 50 mm and the square measures 10 × 10 mm.
- **Material:** white gloss printable vinyl with clear gloss laminate, or white waterslide decal paper sealed with clear coat. If you use an inkjet printer, use inkjet vinyl and let the print dry for about 1 h before laminating.
- Home printers can't print white. On clear vinyl, the "white" areas come out clear and show the case colour.

## Applying

- **Front** = screen side. **L** = the car's left side (+X), **R** = the car's right side (−X), as seen from behind.
- Suggested order: splitter and grille (17, 18, 19), then skirts and sides (5, 1–4), rear (9, 10, 8, 12, 11), wing (6, 7), vent (13), and dome stripes last (14/15 or 16).
- Dome stripes are split into segments because the dome curves in two directions. Lay each segment on the centre line, starting from its middle, and butt the ends together. Warm the vinyl with a hair-dryer and press it outward. If the stripes wrinkle, use the thinner ALT stripes (16).
- Press the vent cover (13) into the slat grooves with a fingernail.
- If you like, finish with a light clear coat. Test it on a spare sticker first.

## Surface prep

- **FDM (PLA/PETG):** lightly sand the layer lines, or prime and paint, then wipe with IPA. Vinyl sticks poorly to rough layer lines.
- **Resin:** wash and fully post-cure the print first, because uncured resin stops the adhesive from sticking. Then lightly sand or prime, and wipe with IPA just before applying.
