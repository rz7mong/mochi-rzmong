## Printing
* Print at **100 % / "Actual size"**, never "Fit to page". Check the 50 mm bar and the 10 × 10 mm square.
* **Material:** white gloss printable vinyl + clear gloss laminate, or white waterslide decal paper sealed with clear coat (most "model kit"-like).
* Home printers can't print white: white text is bare vinyl (white sheet) or see-through (clear sheet).
* Solid pieces have 0.5 mm bleed; floating decals keep a 0.3 mm white margin.
* All text is outlined. The smallest micro-text (0.72–0.95 mm cap height) needs a **≥1200 dpi** printer in best/photo mode; it is decorative only. Main markings (1.9–4.6 mm) stay legible.
* The bottom ~90 mm of the sheet is blank on purpose — trim and reuse.

## Application tips
* **Resin print prep:** wash fully and do a **full post-cure** (tacky resin stops adhesion), lightly sand support marks, prime/paint if wanted, then **wipe with IPA** before applying.
* Dry-fit large pieces (7, 18, 3) with the backing on before peeling.
* Curved dome pieces (14, 15, 16a–d) — lay from the centre outward; a little heat helps. Use decal softener with waterslide. If the 5.5 mm tri-stripe wrinkles, use the ALT single stripes (17B/17C).
* Suggested order: IPA → front (18, 19, 20) → sides (6, 1–5) → rear (10, 11, 9, 13, 12) → wing (7, 8) → vent 14 → V-fin 15 → dome stripes 16/17 → extras 21 → optional light clear coat (test first).
* Front = screen side; mirrored pairs (3–6, 8) R/L; chevrons and triangles always point forward. See `placement_guide.png` for exact positions.
